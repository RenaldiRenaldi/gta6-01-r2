# SOLMARA R2 — The Long Way Home

A fully offline procedural 3D coastal crime story built for make.fun's gta6-01 competition. Marco Reyes returns to Solmara to drive and finds his old crew, the Vela outfit, and a city that remembers him.

Serve with `node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080. Walk and drive with WASD, enter vehicles with E, use Shift for nitro, cycle seasons with T, tune the two radio stations with R, and shop at Palmetto Customs. Take the Coastline Sprint with C and collect a hidden package with X. Q or F calls in a bribe; G repaints a car near Customs. E at the heliport enters the flyable helicopter; E at Casa Reyes saves and cools the heat. The safehouse also stores the crew car. A fresh profile begins on foot beside the Mariner GT.

All city art, weather, music tones, and effects are generated in code. Three.js is vendored for offline use.
